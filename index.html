About

I am a PhD candidate in economics at LEMMA, Université Paris-Panthéon-Assas, supervised by Lucie Ménager and Sidartha Gordon. My work sits at the intersection of applied game theory and political economy: coordination games, global games, and reputation games, applied to the strategic interaction between states and citizens.
